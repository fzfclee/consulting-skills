# Build A Stakeholder Strategy

## Situation

A supplier wants to enter an account served by an incumbent for seven years. The payer, procurement operator, business sponsor, and incumbent hold different incentives and informal influence.

## Inputs

- named stakeholders and formal roles;
- decision and procurement process;
- observed behavior and direct statements;
- relationship strength and uncertainty;
- value proposition, risks, and desired commitment.

## Selected Methods

| Method | Why it changes the decision | Intermediate output | Effect on next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stakeholder-power-map` | Separates formal authority, influence, stance, and hidden vetoes. | Stakeholder power and stance map. | Identifies whose support is necessary and what remains inferred. |
| `account-plan` | Converts the map into value, relationship, evidence, and next-meeting actions. | Account objective, stakeholder moves, proof points, and sequence. | Replaces a generic sales pitch with a staged access and validation plan. |

## Methods Not Used

- Do not use `empathy-map`: motivation can be handled through the evidence-backed stakeholder map.
- Do not use `raci-matrix`: the immediate issue is influence and access, not delivery-role clarity.

## Decision Artifact

First seek permission from the sponsor to run a low-risk service benchmark rather than asking for immediate replacement of the incumbent. Validate the procurement operator's concerns directly. Progress only when both access and a fair comparison mechanism are confirmed.
