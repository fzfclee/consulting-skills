# Clarify An Ambiguous Problem

## Situation

A leadership team says, "The transformation is not working." Interviews contain complaints about governance, adoption, funding, ownership, and delivery speed, but no shared definition of failure.

## Inputs

- leadership and team interview notes;
- delivery and adoption metrics;
- dated decisions and incidents;
- known gaps in data;
- the decision leadership must make in the next four weeks.

## Selected Methods

| Method | Why it changes the decision | Intermediate output | Effect on next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| `evidence-map` | Prevents opinions and repeated claims from becoming facts. | Fact, claim, inference, assumption, and missing-evidence ledger. | Establishes which problem statements can be defended. |
| `issue-tree` | Separates outcomes, causes, constraints, and decisions. | Mutually understandable problem structure. | Identifies the branches that require evidence or action. |

## Methods Not Used

- Do not use `swot-analysis`: the immediate problem is not strategic-position balance.
- Do not use `five-whys-root-cause`: there is not yet one sufficiently bounded problem.

## Decision Artifact

```markdown
### Decision Question
Which two operating constraints most materially prevent adoption and delivery reliability,
and what evidence must be obtained before changing governance?

### Supported Problems
1. Decision ownership changes between planning and delivery.
2. Adoption is measured after release but has no accountable owner.

### Unresolved Claims
1. Funding delay is the primary cause.
2. Teams resist the new operating model.

### Next Evidence
Owner interviews, decision logs, adoption cohorts, and lead-time data.
```
