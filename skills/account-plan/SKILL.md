# Account Plan

Use this skill to run `Account Plan` as a practical consulting method, not as a generic framework explanation.

## Method Notes

- Map decision rights, relationship strength, competitor position, proof gaps, and next ask.
- Do not treat a friendly contact as the account plan.

## Required Inputs

Collect or infer these inputs before execution:

- account background
- opportunity
- stakeholders
- competitors
- relationship evidence

If an input is missing, do not block automatically. Mark it as `missing`, state the assumption used, and add a validation action.

## When Not To Use

Do not use as a sales slogan. Use it to clarify value, buying logic, proof, operating fit, and next commercial action.

## Step-by-Step Execution

| Step | Required input | How to execute | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Set account objective | Account, opportunity, target outcome, timeline. | State win/expand/defend/recover objective and commercial stakes. | Account objective. |
| Map account structure | Stakeholders, decision rights, budget, users, influencers. | Map formal and informal buying network. | Account stakeholder map. |
| Diagnose value and proof | Client business problem, current supplier, competitor, proof assets. | Clarify why change is valuable and what proof is missing. | Value/proof diagnosis. |
| Identify risks and paths | Stakeholder map, competitor position, relationship gaps. | Find blockers, sponsor path, political risks, and fallback routes. | Account risk/path map. |
| Build 30-day plan | Risks, paths, meeting opportunities, owner capacity. | Define next asks, messages, meetings, proof, and validation signals. | 30-day account plan. |

## Quality Gate

- The output must change a decision, action, prioritization, risk view, or validation plan.
- Every major claim must be tied to evidence or labeled as an assumption.
- Each recommendation must name the action, owner or stakeholder, timing, and expected signal.
- Remove framework filler. Do not explain the method unless the explanation helps the user act.
- Keep wording professional and plain enough that a smart non-specialist can use it without translation.
