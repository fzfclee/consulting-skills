# Method Attribution And Source Notes

## Scope

This repository summarizes and operationalizes established methods for use by AI agents. It does not reproduce proprietary books, training materials, diagrams, certification content, or paid templates.

Method names are used descriptively. The skill implementations are original summaries written around required inputs, execution steps, output contracts, evidence discipline, and quality gates.

Consulting Skills is an open-source method library initiated by Li Zhi and may
be used as a public dependency by the O2V Framework. The O2V Framework, CLEAR,
Signal-to-Action, and their related methodology or delivery assets are not part
of this repository and are not licensed under Apache-2.0.

| `balanced-scorecard` | Robert S. Kaplan and David P. Norton, building on earlier performance-measurement work | See the [Balanced Scorecard Institute overview](https://balancedscorecard.org/bsc-basics-overview/). |

| `pre-mortem` | Gary Klein | See ["Performing a Project Premortem"](https://hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem), Harvard Business Review, 2007. |
